Royal Master is one of the two principal degrees conferred in a Council
of Cryptic Masons. Its setting is deliberate and striking: it takes
place before the death of the chief architect of the Temple, so the
candidate hears conversations that look forward to a loss the other
degrees narrate only after the fact — making plain why what will be
lost is worth preserving.

## History

The Cryptic degrees are, in their documented form, the youngest part of
the York Rite. The Royal Master degree developed in the United States
in the early nineteenth century, in the same ferment that produced the
organized Council system itself. Degrees of this character had
circulated among Masons for some years before Councils of Royal and
Select Masters were formally constituted in the 1810s and 1820s, and
when the Councils took shape they gathered these degrees into a coherent
body — one organized around the Temple's secrets and their safekeeping.

The name "Cryptic" itself points to the shared theme: the vault, the
crypt beneath the Temple, and the deposits entrusted there. The Royal
Master degree introduces that theme through the figure of a workman
whose understanding of what must be preserved sets the stage for the
[Select Master degree](/about/degrees/select-master/).

## In the York Rite

Cryptic Masonry is described on the original source as the last part of
the Rite dealing specifically with the Hiramic Legend, and the gateway
to the Temple-restoration tradition. Royal Master opens that gateway;
[Select Master](/about/degrees/select-master/) completes its principal
degrees, and many Councils add
[Super Excellent Master](/about/degrees/super-excellent-master/) as an
honorary degree. See the [Council page](/about/council/) and the
[degree histories](/about/degrees/) index for the whole sequence.
