Super Excellent Master is an honorary degree — it is not one of the two
principal Cryptic degrees and is not required for advancement through
the Rite. Many Councils confer it as an optional honor, and it is
counted among the most affecting ceremonies in the York Rite: its
setting is the fall of Jerusalem and the destruction of Solomon's
Temple, the catastrophe whose loss the Capitular and Cryptic degrees
frame and repair.

## History

The degree is another American development. It emerged in the United
States during the nineteenth century, as the organized Council system
and its neighboring bodies accumulated degrees dramatizing the whole
arc of the Temple story — building, completion, destruction, captivity,
and restoration. As it spread, Councils adopted it as an honorary
ceremony to be conferred on companions of proven standing, rather than
as a required step of the Cryptic system.

Its historical content is drawn from the scriptural accounts of the
Judahite exile — the destruction of the city and Temple and the
carrying away of the people to Babylon. In the York Rite's larger
narrative it supplies the "loss" side of the story at its fullest
scale: what the Royal Arch later recovers, the captivity first
mourned.

## In the York Rite

Super Excellent Master stands alongside
[Royal Master](/about/degrees/royal-master/) and
[Select Master](/about/degrees/select-master/) in many
[Councils of Cryptic Masons](/about/council/), though its honorary
status distinguishes it from the principal degrees. The complete list of
York Rite degree histories is on the [degree histories](/about/degrees/)
page.
